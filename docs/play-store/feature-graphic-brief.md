# Feature Graphic Brief — Gurukula AI (v1.30.0)

Design brief for the Play Store **feature graphic**.

## Requirements

- **Size: 1024 × 500 px** (exact). PNG or JPEG, no alpha.
- Keep important content within a safe area (~centre 900×420) — Play may crop/
  overlay on some surfaces.
- No text near the extreme edges.

## Look & feel

- Gurukula AI **logo** (top-left or centre).
- **Purple / soft student theme** (brand violet ~ `#591EE8` with lighter
  lavender accents); clean, calm, modern Android study-assistant feel.
- One clear headline + one short supporting line.
- Optional: a subtle phone mockup on the right showing a real app screen
  (Home or Summary) — keep it small and uncluttered. If unsure, use a clean
  gradient/pattern instead of a mockup.
- Plenty of breathing room; avoid clutter and busy backgrounds.

## Must avoid

- No Google / Gemini / OpenAI logos or names.
- No "#1", "best", awards, fake ratings, star badges, or "new/free/sale".
- No misleading claims (e.g. "100% offline", "guaranteed grades").
- No screenshots of real personal data.

## Headline options (pick 1)

1. **Your private study space**
2. **Notes into summaries, flashcards & quizzes**
3. **Study smarter, privately**

## Subtitle options (pick 1)

1. Offline-focused. On-device where supported.
2. Summaries, flashcards, quizzes & a planner — on your device.
3. Private, local-first study assistant for students.

## Suggested pairing

Headline **1** + Subtitle **1** for the clearest, most honest message; or
Headline **2** + Subtitle **2** to lead with features. Keep the total text
short and legible at small sizes.
