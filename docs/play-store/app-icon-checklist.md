# App Icon Checklist — Gurukula AI (v1.30.0)

For the Play Store **high-res app icon**. (Do not generate the icon here — this
is a checklist for producing/exporting it.)

## Requirements

- **512 × 512 px**, **32-bit PNG** (with alpha allowed by Play for the store
  icon). No rounded-corner masking baked in — Play applies its own shape.
- Under 1 MB.
- Must **match the in-app launcher icon / logo** (`assets/logo/app_launcher_icon.png`)
  so the store and installed app look consistent.
- Legible at small sizes (test at ~48 px): a simple, bold "g"/Gurukula mark on
  the brand violet reads better than the full wordmark.

## Must avoid

- No misleading badges or overlays ("free", "sale", "new", "#1", "best").
- No fake notification dots, star ratings, or award badges.
- No Google / Gemini / OpenAI marks.
- No text that duplicates the app name unnecessarily (the name shows beside it).

## Export checklist

- [ ] Start from the existing brand mark used for the launcher adaptive icon.
- [ ] Export a **512×512 PNG** with the brand violet background
      (adaptive background `#5718F0` is used in-app).
- [ ] Verify it visually matches the installed launcher icon.
- [ ] Check legibility at 48 px and 96 px.
- [ ] Confirm no badges/claims are present.
- [ ] Keep a master (vector/high-res) copy for future regeneration.

## Notes

The in-app launcher icon is generated by `flutter_launcher_icons` from
`assets/logo/app_launcher_icon.png` (adaptive foreground/background configured in
`pubspec.yaml`). The Play **store** icon is uploaded separately in Play Console
and should be derived from the same source art.
