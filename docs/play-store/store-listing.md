## C. Full description

> Play limit is 4000 characters. This draft is well under it.

```
Gurukula AI is a privacy-first study assistant for students. Turn your own
notes into summaries, flashcards, quizzes, study packs and revision tools — with
your study data kept on your device.

Bring your notes in the way that suits you: paste text, import a TXT file or a
text-based PDF, pick an image from your gallery, or scan printed notes with your
camera. On-device text recognition (OCR) reads the text so you can start
studying.

From a single note you can:
• Generate a clear summary — choose Short, Medium or Detailed.
• Create flashcards — Quick revision or Exam prep style.
• Build a quiz — Easy, Medium or Hard — and test yourself.
• Practise with revision mode and spaced review.
• Export a Study Pack (.txt) to save or share.

Plan your studying with the built-in Study Planner: add exams or goals, set a
target date, and see how many days you have left and how ready you feel.

Privacy is built in:
• Encrypted Storage protects your saved study data at rest on your device.
• Privacy Lock can require a PIN or biometric unlock to open the app.
• Your study notes and generated materials stay on your device — nothing is
  synced to a server. They only leave the app if you choose to export or share
  them.
• You can delete your account or clear your local study data from inside the
  app at any time.

About the AI: Gurukula AI uses on-device AI where your device supports it. When
on-device AI is not available, it uses a built-in local fallback so the study
tools keep working. Generated study materials are a helpful starting point and
may contain mistakes — always check important content against your course
materials.

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. Google Sign-In is available, and
email/password sign-in is available where enabled. Sign-in is for your account
only; it is not used to upload your study content.

Gurukula AI does not use OpenAI, does not use a cloud Gemini API, has no cloud
study sync, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

Made for students who want a calm, private, offline-focused way to revise.
```
