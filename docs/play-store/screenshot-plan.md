# Screenshot Plan — Gurukula AI (v1.30.0)

Plan for 8 phone screenshots for the Play Store listing. Use **real app
screenshots** with **demo content only** — never real personal data.

## Technical requirements

- Format: **PNG or JPEG**, 24-bit, no alpha for the store upload.
- Phone screenshots: 16:9 or 9:16, min 320 px, max 3840 px on each side; Play
  accepts 2–8. Capture on a real device or emulator at a common phone size
  (e.g. 1080×2340).
- Use **real app UI** — no misleading mockups or fake features.
- Keep the **status bar clean** (full battery, no notifications; enable demo/
  "clean status bar" mode if available, or crop it).
- Overlay text must be **readable** (high contrast, large enough on a phone).
- **Do not show** personal email, account name, or private study data — sign in
  with a throwaway demo account and use demo notes.
- Consistent purple theme and framing across all shots.

## Honest overlay wording

Use: "Private study space", "Offline-focused", "Local-first", "On-device where
supported", "Fallback-safe". Avoid: "100% offline", "Cloud-free AI on every
phone", "Guaranteed grades", "Fully secure", "Gemini Nano on all devices".

## The 8 screenshots

### 1. Auth Landing
- **Capture:** Auth Landing screen (logo, Log in / Sign up / Continue with Google).
- **Title:** Your private study space
- **Subtitle:** Sign in and keep your notes on your device
- **Communicates:** Privacy-first, simple entry.
- **Setup:** Sign out first so the landing shows.

### 2. Home / workspace overview
- **Capture:** Home tab (greeting, tools, progress, revision).
- **Title:** Everything for revision, in one place
- **Subtitle:** Summaries, flashcards, quizzes and a planner
- **Communicates:** Breadth of study tools.
- **Setup:** Demo account with a couple of demo notes so cards aren't empty.

### 3. Note workspace (Note tab)
- **Capture:** Study workspace Note tab showing a demo note + tabs (Note / Summary / Tools / Flashcards / Quiz).
- **Title:** From your notes to study tools
- **Subtitle:** One note becomes summaries, cards and quizzes
- **Communicates:** The core note → tools flow.
- **Setup:** Create a demo note titled e.g. "Photosynthesis".

### 4. Summary generation with options
- **Capture:** Summary tab with the Short / Medium / Detailed selector visible and a generated summary.
- **Title:** Summaries your way
- **Subtitle:** Choose Short, Medium or Detailed
- **Communicates:** Control over output; honest "generated" framing.
- **Setup:** Generate a summary on the demo note.

### 5. Flashcards or quiz generation
- **Capture:** Flashcards tab (Quick revision / Exam prep chips) OR Quiz tab (Easy/Medium/Hard) with generated content.
- **Title:** Flashcards and quizzes in seconds
- **Subtitle:** Quick revision or exam-prep style
- **Communicates:** Active recall tools.
- **Setup:** Generate flashcards/quiz on the demo note.

### 6. Study Planner
- **Capture:** Study Planner list with 1–2 demo goals showing days-remaining + status.
- **Title:** Plan your exams
- **Subtitle:** Track goals and days remaining
- **Communicates:** Exam/goal planning.
- **Setup:** Add demo goals like "Biology final" with a future date.

### 7. Privacy Lock & Encrypted Storage
- **Capture:** Profile → Privacy Lock settings (showing Encrypted Storage status + Privacy Lock toggle) OR the Lock screen.
- **Title:** Privacy built in
- **Subtitle:** Privacy Lock + Encrypted Storage on your device
- **Communicates:** Security posture (honest, at-rest).
- **Setup:** Enable Privacy Lock to show the "On" state / lock screen.

### 8. Export Study Pack
- **Capture:** Note tab "Export study pack" action or the share sheet with a Study Pack file.
- **Title:** Take your notes anywhere
- **Subtitle:** Export a Study Pack to save or share
- **Communicates:** Ownership/portability of study content.
- **Setup:** Trigger Export study pack on the demo note.

## Capture order tip

Set up the demo account + one demo note ("Photosynthesis" or "Deadlocks") and a
couple of planner goals first, then capture 3→8 in one session so content is
consistent. Capture 1 (Auth Landing) after signing out.
