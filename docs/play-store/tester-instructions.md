# Gurukula AI — Tester Instructions

Thanks for helping test **Gurukula AI**! This takes about 10–15 minutes.
(You can copy/paste this to testers; replace `<OPT_IN_LINK>` with your Play
opt-in link.)

## What is Gurukula AI?

A privacy-first Android study assistant. Turn your notes into summaries,
flashcards, quizzes and study packs, and plan your exams. Your study data stays
on your device.

## 1. Join the test

1. Open the opt-in link on your Android phone: **`<OPT_IN_LINK>`**
2. Tap **Become a tester / Accept**.
3. Tap the link to **install Gurukula AI from Google Play**. (If it says
   "not available", wait a few minutes after opting in, or update the Play
   Store app.)

## 2. Please test these (in order)

1. **Open the app** — it should reach the sign-in (Auth Landing) screen.
2. **Sign in with Google** ("Continue with Google").
   - *Email/password sign up/login is available only if we've enabled it in
     Firebase. If email shows "not enabled yet", just use Google — that's
     expected.*
3. **Complete your student profile** (study level, subject, goal; institution
   optional).
4. **Onboarding** — read or **Skip**.
5. **Add a text note:** Paste text → paste any sample paragraph → save.
   - *Use demo/sample content only — please don't paste private or sensitive
     notes during testing.*
6. **Generate a summary** (try Short / Medium / Detailed).
7. **Generate flashcards** (Quick revision / Exam prep).
8. **Generate a quiz** (Easy / Medium / Hard) and take it.
9. **Study Planner:** add a goal with a future date.
10. **Export Study Pack (.txt)** from the Note tab.
11. **Privacy Lock:** Profile → Privacy Lock → enable, set a PIN, reopen the app
    to unlock; then disable it.
12. **Clear local study data:** Profile → Privacy & data (keeps your account).
13. **Delete test account:** Profile → Danger zone → **Delete account** when
    you're finished (see below).

## Good to know (not bugs)

- **On-device AI may show "Not supported"** on some phones. The app then uses
  **fallback mode** — summaries/flashcards/quizzes still work. This is expected.
- **OCR** (camera/gallery import) works best on **clear printed English text**;
  blurry or handwritten text may not read well.

## Report a bug

Please fill in the feedback form: **`<FEEDBACK_FORM_LINK>`** (or email
**skausian@gmail.com**). Include:

- **What happened** and **what you expected**.
- **Steps to reproduce**.
- **Device model** (e.g. Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra).
- **Android version** (Settings → About phone).
- **App version** (Profile → About Gurukula → "Version …").
- **Screenshot or short screen recording** if possible.
- Whether the app **crashed** or got **stuck on a screen**.

## Delete your test account when done

Profile → **Danger zone → Delete account** → type **DELETE** → (Google users may
re-sign-in to confirm) → you'll return to the sign-in screen. This removes your
account and all local data on the device.

Thank you! 🙌
